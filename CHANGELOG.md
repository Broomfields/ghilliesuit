# Changelog

Changes to the mod are listed here. Versions before 1.1.0 are covered in the GitHub release notes.

## [1.1.0] - Unreleased

### Added

- **Comfort Enchantment** can be applied to any ghillie piece. Each enchanted piece adds 5 seconds of active stealth from a full hunger bar. A full Comfort set gives you 30 seconds.
- **Poise Enchantment** can be applied to the ghillie tunic. Fully charged melee attacks become critical strikes while actively hidden.
- **Mending Enchantment** and the **Unbreaking Enchantment** can be applied to every ghillie piece. Both keep their vanilla effects and can coexist with the suit's other enchantments.
- **Swift Sneak Enchantment** can be applied to the ghillie pants.
- Comfort, Poise, and Unbreaking are available at the enchanting table. Mending and Swift Sneak can be applied using an enchanted book and an anvil.

### Changed

- Active stealth now drains your hunger bar and stops when it is empty. A full hunger bar gives you about 10 seconds without Comfort.
- Comfort and Poise cannot share a tunic. Other vanilla armour enchantments, such as Protection, remain unavailable.
- Expanded gameplay tests to cover hunger drain, enchantment effects, compatibility, and enchanting table offers.
